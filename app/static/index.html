Data insights

Feature 1: age group

Children aged 5 to 15 make up 63.7% of the 12,560 clean images, which suits an education platform for school children. Engaged and disengaged images are almost equal inside every age group, so age alone cannot predict engagement.

Feature 2: gender within age group

Boys and girls are equal at ages 5 to 15, but only 300 images of boys aged 16 to 21 remain, because 85% of that folder was copies of the same photos. Girls make up 61% of the clean data.

What the data tells us: the dataset is built around school-age children and is balanced by label in every age and gender group, while simple image properties such as brightness carry almost no information about the label. A model that scores well must therefore use the learner's posture, gaze and activity. Its weak points are copied files (removed before training), fewer older boys, and photos taken in sessions of about 20, which is why the data was split by session.
